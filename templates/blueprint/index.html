THE VELLUM HOUSE

A single-storey dwelling drawn to the inch: load-bearing calm, daylight from the north, and nothing on the sheet that isn't holding something up. This set describes it in full — plans, elevations, sections, and the small details that decide whether a door swings true.

DRAWING SCHEDULE

A-01

Floor Plans

Room by room at 1:50 — swing of every door, run of every pipe, tiled to the nearest 100mm. The house, lying flat and honest.

Elevations

Four faces, all cardinal. Cladding coursed, cill heights ticked, the north glass drawn wide to drink the flat grey light.

Cut clean through the ridge. Here the roof pitch argues with the headroom and, for once, both win. Insulation hatched, not hidden.
