Season of MCMXXV

An Evening Without Equal

Beneath a ceiling of hammered gold the orchestra strikes its first chord, the lift doors part upon marble, and the night unfurls like a fan. Dine, dance, and retire to suites that overlook the whole electric city. Here extravagance is merely the house standard.

Reserved exclusively for members & their guests

The Grand Ballroom

A gilded cathedral of dance, floored in black-and-cream marble and crowned by a sunburst dome. The band plays until the small hours.

The Gilded Theatre

Eight hundred velvet seats beneath a stepped proscenium of leafed gold. Revues, operettas, and a midnight cabaret that is never advertised.

The Midnight Supper Club

Oysters, champagne towers, and quail under glass served until dawn. The bartender remembers your drink before you do.

The Observation Suites

Forty storeys up, where the skyline becomes your private frieze. Silk, steam heat, and a telephone that summons any indulgence.

The Society Pages

This Saturday the Sovereign hosts the Peacock Ball in aid of the Metropolitan Opera, white tie and plumage encouraged. The Vanderleigh party takes the Rooftop Terrace at ten. Our resident astronomer opens the observation deck to members at midnight, weather permitting the stars.
