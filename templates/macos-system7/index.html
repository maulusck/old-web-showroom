The Foundry

A two-person design shop running happily on System 7.

Welcome to Macintosh.

We set posters, cut logotypes, and letter the occasional record sleeve — all on a beige box the size of a bread bin. Double-click a folder below to rummage through the drawers. Nothing here is faster than a 68030, and we wouldn't have it any other way.

Please empty the Trash before you leave, and remember to hold down the mouse button when choosing from a menu. The machine remembers everything; the humans, less so.
