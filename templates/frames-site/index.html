Welcome to our little corner of the night sky!

Pour yourself some coffee, dim the lights, and let your eyes adjust. The Dark Sky Society is a club of amateur stargazers who meet on the first clear Friday of every month out at Culver's Ridge, far from the streetlights. We drag out the Dobsonians, swap eyepieces, argue about collimation, and point at faint smudges until dawn. No membership fee — just bring a red flashlight and a thermos. This page is hand-coded by our webmaster in Notepad, so mind the dust.
