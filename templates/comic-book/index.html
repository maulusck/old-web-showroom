MEANWHILE, HIGH ABOVE MERIDIAN CITY…

The sirens had been screaming for an hour. Down below, nine million souls held their breath — and one streak of red fire answered the call.

Dispatch to Comet — the reactor's gone critical! You're our last hope!
THE ROOFTOP

Clark — mild-mannered no longer — tears open his collar. The badge beneath catches the moonlight.

If I'm one second late… this whole city is a crater.
DEEP IN THE REACTOR CORE…

And there he waits — DOCTOR FALLOUT, his reactor-suit drinking the light, grinning behind a visor of cracked lead glass.

TOO LATE, COMET! By midnight this city glows — and I'll be the only shadow left!
KRAKOW!
THE CLASH

Fist meets lead. The core-room buckles. For one blinding instant, the whole sky over Meridian turns to daylight.

ZZRAKK!
THE COOLDOWN

He slams the control rods home with his bare hands. The gauge falls. The hum dies. The city — still here — exhales.

Reactor's cold, Doctor. Looks like the shadows lose tonight.
…AND SO, ANOTHER DAWN!

He is gone before the first reporter arrives — a red streak against a pink sky, already listening for the next cry for help.
