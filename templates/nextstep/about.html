Release Notes
Version 3.3

What changed, and why it matters.

Read top to bottom. The system got faster, the Dock got smarter, and the printer finally stopped arguing with the screen.

Kernel & Shell

The Mach micro-kernel now schedules threads with half the overhead, so a full compile runs while Mail still answers on the first click. The shell ships with the compiler toolchain wired in — no second install, no path surgery.

Mail & the Dock

Drag any application to the Dock and it survives the next login. Unread mail lifts a small flag on its tile; you never miss it from across the desk. Voice attachments record straight from the microphone port.

Display & Print

Display PostScript means the screen and the Laser Printer draw from one language. Preview a letter, send it to the tray, and the paper matches the glass to the pixel. What you see is, at last, what you get.

Filed in the Workspace under /NextLibrary/Documentation. Earlier notes are archived beside this one; the Dock keeps the latest within reach. When you are done, choose Log Out from the menu — the session returns exactly as you left it.
