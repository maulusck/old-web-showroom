OUTFITTING

Canvas, khaki, and nothing you'll have to apologise for.

The Depot kits every Meridian crew from the skin out. If it can't take a sandstorm in the morning and a swim in the afternoon, it doesn't make the shelf.

The Trophy Jacket

Waxed cotton, bellows pockets, collar that stands up to the wind. Breaks in by day two and never lets go. Sand, rust, and khaki.

Depot Trousers

Double-knee canvas that dries on the ride and takes a crease for the bar. Cut loose enough to kneel in the dust and change a tyre.

Lagoon Shirt

Light blue poplin, long enough to keep the sun off, open enough to drink in. The one you'll be wearing in every photo from the trip.

Saltwater Boots

Oiled leather up top, grip rubber below, laces you can trust with both feet off the pegs. Resoled free for the life of the boot.

The Field Kit

Canteen, compass, tide card, and a tin that survives being sat on. Everything the Logbook tells you to carry, in one canvas roll.

Deck Towel

Oversized, sand-shedding, Meridian stripe down the middle. Doubles as a groundsheet, a sunshade, and a flag for the slow riders.

FITTING ROOM

Measured once, outfitted for good.

Book any expedition and your fitting comes with it. Walk in for a kit list, walk out for the pool — the Depot keeps your sizes on file for next season.
