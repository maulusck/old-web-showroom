SEASON 2026

Five routes. One cold drink at the end of each.

Every Meridian run is scouted twice before you ride it once — once in the dry season and once in the wet, so the map in your hands is the map under your tyres. Dust optional, sunset guaranteed.

The Long Dune — 6 days

Inland crossing from the oasis depot to the high ridge. Soft sand, hard stars, radio check at every camp. Ends at the coast with your boots full of grit.

Saltline Run — 3 days

A quick blast along the old salt road to the lagoon. Good first expedition: short enough to learn the rig, long enough to earn the swim.

Monsoon Loop — 8 days

Wet-season circuit for crews who like the hard version. River fords, mud recovery drills, and a hot shower waiting that you will have earned.

Coast-to-Coast — 11 days

The full traverse, depot to depot, ocean to ocean. Booked a year out and worth every month of the wait. Twelve riders, two guides, no shortcuts.

BEFORE YOU GO

Kitted at the Depot, briefed at the bar.

Every booking includes a fitting at the Depot and a route briefing over something tall and cold. Turn up a day early — the pool is open to riders.
