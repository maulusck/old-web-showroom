SHE RAN OUT OF NIGHT BEFORE SHE RAN OUT OF ENEMIES

They buried the body before the coffee went cold. By dawn the only witness left breathing was a diner waitress with a run in her stocking and a secret she didn't know she was carrying. The cops wanted her for murder. The Syndicate wanted her for good. And the one man who could clear her name was lying face-down in the harbor with her phone number in his pocket.

From the first scream to the last gunshot, DAME IN THE DARK drags you down every wet alley in a city that eats its own. Vincent “Slug” Malory writes the way a switchblade opens — fast, bright, and already too late to stop. You will not put it down. You will not sleep. You will check the lock twice.

“Malory doesn't write chapters. He writes confessions.” — THE MIDNIGHT LEDGER
