DAME IN THE DARK

She knew too much — and the city knew her name…

by VINCENT “SLUG” MALORY

“A thrill on every page”

Carla Reyes clocked out of the all-night diner at 3 a.m. and clocked into a nightmare. One wrong booth, one dropped envelope, one dead man in a camel coat — and suddenly every taxi was following her and every phone was ringing in an empty room. She had sixty cents, a borrowed pistol, and until sunrise to prove she wasn't the one who pulled the trigger. They said nobody beats the Syndicate. Carla never was much for being told.
