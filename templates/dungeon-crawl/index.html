-- quest --

Retrieve the Amulet of Yendor from Dlvl 26.

Twenty-six floors stand between your boots and the Amulet. Rats first, then worse. The stairs only go one way until you're carrying the thing nobody has carried back out alive. Rations are short, the gods are watching, and the corridors remember the last fool who ran.

The Oracle, consulted (fee: 200 zm)

"Beware the floor that counts your steps. Eat the lichen, not the corpse four days old. When the shopkeeper says ‘pay before you leave,’ he means it, and he is faster than you." The Oracle then falls silent. You feel you got your money's worth, roughly.

@ you d k r dog / kobold / rat $ gold | - + wall / door . floor # corridor

Pack — press the letter to use:

a) a +1 short sword (wielded)

Chipped, honest, and already bloodied tonight. It will not win songs, but it has never jammed.

b) 4 food rations

Grey, dense, flavourless. Enough to reach Dlvl 9 if you stop gloating and keep moving.

c) an unidentified scroll

Labelled "VELOX NEB". Could be teleport. Could be fire. Read it in a room with the door open.

d) a blessed +0 ring mail

Heavy enough to slow the rot, light enough to swim in. Smells faintly of the smith who wept over it.

e) 2 potions (murky)

One fizzes, one does not. Tradition holds you drink the fizzy one first and apologise later.

f) a tin whistle

Summons the dog, mostly. Occasionally summons attention you did not want. Worth the gamble.
