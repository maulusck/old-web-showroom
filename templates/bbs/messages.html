Subj: USR Courier won't negotiate V.32bis
Date: Tue 28 Feb 1995  21:50   Area: 02/TechTalk   Reads: 37

Pulled a USR Courier HST Dual Standard out of a parts bin and it answers fine, but every caller drops to 9600 — it flat refuses to climb to V.32bis. Dialed the board from a second line and watched the handshake: it trains, backs off, trains again, then gives up and settles. S-register voodoo, or is the ROM just too old to know the trick? I've tried AT&F then ATB0, no joy. The line itself is clean — 14.4 on the Supra next to it, same wall jack.

Before anyone says it: yes, I reseated the DAA board. Twice. Reply here or netmail me — I'll be lurking the base till the dragon respawns. — Static
