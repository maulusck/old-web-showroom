Everything prints twice,
nothing lines up, it’s perfect.

We run two drums and two passes: fluorescent pink, then teal, each a hair out of register. Where they overlap the inks multiply into a bruised third colour nobody mixed on purpose. The grain is real, the dots are showing, and we wouldn’t trade a single misfeed for a clean laser print.
