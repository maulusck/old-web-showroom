Threw the last of the sunflower hearts out at dawn and — there he was. A male Rose-breasted Grosbeak, that crisp black-and-white tuxedo with the rosy bib, bold as anything on the platform feeder.

Right on schedule too; my logbook has him arriving the first week of May three years running. Something very reassuring about that. Anyone else getting them north of the river yet?

Camera was charging, naturally. I'll be lurking by the window all afternoon.

MapleFinch wrote:
Right on schedule too; my logbook has him arriving the first week of May three years running.

Beautiful! Nothing beats that first spring male. We had two chasing each other around the lilac this morning — the females usually turn up about a week behind, so keep that platform stocked.

If you want them to linger, a half-orange on a spike works wonders. For the logbook keepers, here's the shorthand I use:

05-07 RBGR 1 ad. male, platform feeder, 0612h wx: clear, light SW wind, 7°C note: singing from the ash by 0640h

Lovely sighting. Moving this to the top of the Daily Sightings board so the regulars see it.
