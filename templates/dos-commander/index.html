WELCOME TO DOS COMMANDER

Two panels, one glowing bar, and the whole drive at your fingertips. No mouse required, no mercy given. Point the cursor, hit a key, watch the bytes fly. This is file management the way the ancients meant it.

── HOTKEY FIELD GUIDE ──

  • F5 — COPY Blast files from the left panel to the right at 115,200 baud of pure confidence.
  • F3 — VIEW Peek inside any file without launching a thing. Flip to hex if you're feeling brave.
  • TAB — SWAP Throw the cursor bar across to the other drive and back. The panels never argue.
  • ALT-F1 — DRIVE Swap the floppy, scan the hard card, mount the CD-ROM caddy. Everything's a letter.
