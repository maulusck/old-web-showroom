Learn the Pointers First

Find the Big Dipper, follow its lip five lengths, and you’ve met Polaris. From there the whole sky unfolds. Three cards worth of sign-posts below.

Orion, the Hunter

The three belt stars are your anchor. Hang your gaze below them to the fuzzy sword — that smudge is the Great Nebula, a nursery of new stars.
