A design practice, est. 1987

Nothing
decorative.
Everything
deliberate.

We set the grid, then break only what the message demands. Identity, editorial, type and signage for people who distrust ornament. No gradients. No drop shadows. No apologies.

Manifest

A page is a system before it is a surface. Begin with the column, the baseline, the hairline that keeps order; let content fill the structure instead of hiding it. White space is not emptiness — it is the loudest element on the page. When in doubt, remove.

Disciplines — 01 / 04

01

Identity

Marks, systems and rules thin enough to survive every medium. Built from a grid, documented to the pixel.

02

Editorial

Books, catalogues and reports where the typography does the arguing and the margins do the breathing.

03

Type

Letterforms drawn for a single job and no other. Severe, legible, calibrated to the column they will live in.

04

Signage

Wayfinding that trusts an arrow and a word. Painted direct onto concrete where the budget allows.
