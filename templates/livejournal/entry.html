Thursday, November 13th, 2003 · 11:48 pm

the radiator finally kicked in

So it took the super three weeks and one very passive-aggressive note taped to the boiler room door, but the heat is ON and I have not taken off this one enormous grey cardigan in four days. This is who I am now. A person in a cardigan, typing.

Spent most of tonight reorganizing my mp3 folder instead of writing the paper due Monday, which is the most 2003 sentence I have ever produced. Found a live bootleg I forgot I had. Cried a little. Normal Thursday.

note to self: future me, if you are reading this, did you ever finish the paper? be honest.
