The 32-bit desktop that does it all — presentation-manager smooth. Objects: 6

Drag it, drop it, Warp it.

Everything on this desktop is a live object. Open a folder, drag a program onto the LaunchPad, drop a document on a printer — the Workplace Shell remembers where you left it. Crash-protected multitasking, eight ways from Tuesday. Welcome back to work.

Programs

A folder of installed apps, each a tidy icon. Double-click to launch; right-click for its settings notebook.

Drives

Your HPFS and FAT volumes. Browse C:, format a diskette, or peek at the 1.2GB whopper you just installed.

Printers

Drop a document straight onto the printer object. Queue it, pause it, reorder the jobs — all by dragging.

Network

Peer-to-peer shares and the wider wires beyond. Map a neighbor's drive, chat over NetBIOS, dial the world.

System Setup

Colors, fonts, mouse, sound schemes. Open the notebook, flip the tabs, and bend the desktop to your will.

Information

The online Command Reference and tutorials. Everything the manual says, minus the three-ring binder.
