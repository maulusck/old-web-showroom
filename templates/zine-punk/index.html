THREE CHORDS & A GRUDGE

Four bands. One busted PA. The back room of the laundromat on Ash St, doors at 8, cops by 11. Bring your own earplugs and a buck for the photocopier fund. No posers, no managers, no refunds. Xerox this flyer & staple it everywhere.

THE DIAL TONES

Headliners. Two minutes a song, forty seconds of feedback. Last seen setting a snare drum on fire in a basement off Route 9.

WET CEMENT

Faster than you can read this sentence. They owe everyone money and that is somehow part of the charm. Merch is a Sharpie on your arm.

GUTTER MATH

Three members, one working amp, zero chill. Their demo is a cassette dubbed over somebody's aunt's wedding video. Play it loud.

GIRLS IN THE VENTS

Opening at 8 sharp whether or not the mic works. Half the set is a rant about the rent. The other half is better than most records.

CUT · PASTE · PHOTOCOPY · REPEAT

We make this zine on a stolen copier at 2am and we are not sorry. If the ink is smudged it means a human touched it. Mail us your scene reports, your bad poetry, your worse band — glue-stick and a grudge, that is the whole method. Stay loud.
