Four lines. One crossing. Mind the gap.

The diagram below is schematic: every corner is a true 45° or right angle, every station a tick on the run. Distances lie on purpose so the junctions tell the truth. You are standing at Kestrel Cross — the only point where every colour meets.

Route Key

  • Harbour Line

    Saltmarket to Marrow Quay, tracing the old waterfront. Trains every four minutes; last boat-connection at half eleven.

  • Vermillion Line

    The spine — North Gate down through the market to the foundries at Foundry End. Steepest grade on the network; hold the rail.

  • Orchard Line

    Garland Hill to Pelican Yard, the long green way round the river flats. Runs above ground past the allotments in summer.

  • Lantern Line

    Tidelock through the theatre district to Copperworks. Night service only — follow the lamps, not the clock.

Featured Stops

  • Kestrel Cross ◉

    Every line meets here under the glass vault. Four platforms, one clock, and a busker who only knows three songs.

  • Elderford Junction

    Change for Harbour and Orchard. Cross the footbridge; the eastbound platform is the one that smells of coffee.

  • Candleford

    Vermillion meets Lantern beneath the chandlery. Mind the low arch between the running lamps.

  • Beacon Hill

    Orchard and Lantern, highest stop on the map. On a clear night you can see the whole diagram light up below.
