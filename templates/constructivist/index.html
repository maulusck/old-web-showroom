EXHIBITION NO. 5 — OPEN TO ALL WHO WORK

FORM IS A TOOL. BUILD THE FUTURE OUT LOUD.

We do not decorate — we construct. The poster is a machine, the page is a plan, the line is a worker. Red and black against honest cream: no ornament, no apology. Every angle does labour; every circle turns the wheel. Bring your hands to the press.

01

THE PRINT SHOP

Hand-set type, heavy rule, and the diagonal as a working axis. We cut posters the way the lathe cuts steel — straight, loud, and for use.

02

THE DRAWING FLOOR

Compositions on the grid and against it. Circle, wedge, bar: the alphabet of construction, drilled until the hand builds without thinking.

03

THE EXHIBITION HALL

Walls given over to the collective. No signatures, no masters — only the work and the worker who shaped it. Doors open each week at the whistle.

04

THE PRESSES

Ink by the barrel, paper by the ream. Bring a design and leave with a thousand. Production is the proof: a poster unseen is a tool unused.

FIVE LINES OF THE WORKSHOP

We reject the decorative and the precious. Design is not a mirror held to the ordering class — it is a lever in the hand of the maker. The right angle is a promise; the diagonal, a shove. We print in red because red is the colour of the forge, and in black because black is the weight of the type. What cannot be built should not be drawn. What is built belongs to all who built it. Sign nothing; stamp everything.
