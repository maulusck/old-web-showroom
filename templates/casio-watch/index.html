Timekeeping

Hold a steady second since 1991. The hourly beep is off by default, the hands never were — because there are none. Accurate to ±30 seconds a month, which on a watch this cheap feels like a small miracle. Press MODE to cycle; LIGHT burns the little green bulb for exactly one impatient second.

Daily Alarm

One alarm. One beep pattern. No snooze, no mercy, no app. Set it with ADJUST, confirm with MODE, then forget it exists until 06:30 reminds you loudly that you made a decision last night. The tiny bell glyph lights when armed — the only commitment this watch asks of you.

Stopwatch

1/100-second chronograph that counts for an hour before it shrugs and starts over. SPLIT freezes the lap without stopping the clock — the one genuinely clever trick in a watch that cost less than lunch. Time your eggs, your intervals, your excuses. It will not judge the result, only record it.

Dual Time

A second time zone for the version of you that is somewhere else. Home stays on the main line; the other city lives here, one button away, so you always know whether it is polite to call. Set the offset once and it keeps both honest — no roaming charges, no daylight-saving apology emails.
